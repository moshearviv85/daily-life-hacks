## Density isn't a pile of fiber

The [highest-fiber fast-food check](/high-fiber-fast-food-options-guide/) still belongs to a custom Chipotle veggie bowl at 25 g of fiber. That bowl is 725 calories, so it doesn't get a row here. Eighteen grams in 350 calories beats 25 grams in 725 calories on this particular score. That's what dividing is for.

If you have the calories and you want the biggest fiber number, read that other page. If the window is 500 calories, the soup is the dense one. The [orders that clear 20 grams of protein and 10 grams of fiber](/fast-food-meals-with-protein-and-fiber/) are a third list. Several of them are over 500 calories, and the bean burrito never makes that list because 13 g of protein and 8 g of fiber miss both gates. It makes this one.

Panera's wraps are the overlap that actually fits. Green Goddess is 14 g of fiber and 32 g of protein at 460 calories. Caesar matches the grams at 480 calories. Frontega is 12 g and 30 g at 450 calories, all in the [Panera guide](https://www.panerabread.com/content/dam/panerabread/documents/c8-26-nutrition-guide.pdf). Same neighborhood. Caesar is the salty cousin.

## Under 500 calories doesn't mean the sodium left

The [Dietary Guidelines for Americans 2020-2025](https://www.dietaryguidelines.gov/sites/default/files/2020-12/Dietary_Guidelines_for_Americans_2020-2025.pdf) tell adults to stay under 2,300 mg of sodium a day. The [FDA Daily Value](https://www.fda.gov/food/nutrition-facts-label/daily-value-nutrition-and-supplement-facts-labels) uses that same 2,300 mg on the label. Neither number is a prescription with your name on it.

The soup is still 1,115 mg of sodium on the [Chick-fil-A table](https://www.chick-fil-a.com/nutrition-allergens). That's a lot of a label-day in one cup, even at 5.1 g of fiber per 100 calories. Panera's Caesar wrap is 1,510 mg. Starbucks plain oatmeal is the quiet row: 4 g of fiber, 160 calories, 125 mg of sodium, 2.5 g per 100 calories. Panera's steel-cut oatmeal is 7 g of fiber, 330 calories, and 170 mg of sodium. Dunkin's multigrain bagel is 8 g of fiber at 380 calories and 550 mg of sodium. The oatmeals win the salt column. They don't win the density column.

## What I'd actually order from this list

If the job is fiber per calorie, get the soup. If the job is protein in the same bag, get a Panera wrap, and take Frontega when the sodium column matters. If you want the sodium under 200 mg, the two oatmeals are the only rows that qualify.

[Wendy's chili cheese potato](https://api.app.prd.wendys.digital/web-client-gateway/menu/getSiteMenu?lang=en&cntry=US&sourceCode=ORDER.WENDYS.COM&version=23.1.0&siteNum=0) has 21 g of protein and 8 g of fiber, and it's still last on density at 1.6 g per 100 calories, with 490 calories and 830 mg of sodium. It made the cutoff. It didn't win it. Large guacamole is 12 g of fiber and 4 g of protein. Fine next to something. Silly as the whole meal.
