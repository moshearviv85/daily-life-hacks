Fast-food fiber lists age badly. Menus change, portions move, and a gram count without the exact build is trivia wearing a nutrition label.

Answer up front: the highest-fiber custom order below is a Chipotle veggie bowl with both beans at **25 grams of fiber**. Of the 29 orders verified against official chain data on November 23, 2026, no fixed menu item wins outright. Subway's Veggie Patty Protein Bowl carries the most fiber at 19 grams, but that's 540 calories, 1,550 milligrams of sodium, and participating locations only. Cava's Falafel Crunch bowl and Chick-fil-A's Chicken Tortilla Soup both land at 18 grams.

The [FDA Daily Value for dietary fiber is 28 grams](https://www.fda.gov/food/nutrition-facts-label/daily-value-nutrition-and-supplement-facts-labels), and 20% or more of it counts as a high amount. The FDA's label guide calls fiber a [dietary component of public health concern](https://www.accessdata.fda.gov/scripts/InteractiveNutritionFactsLabel/assets/InteractiveNFL_DietaryFiber_October2021.pdf) because most Americans fall short. One column isn't a grade for the whole meal.

## Which fast food order has the most fiber?

Chipotle, but only with the full build above. Skip either bean and the headline drops. The sofritas bowl, one bean instead of two, is 19 grams at 740 calories. Large guacamole by itself is 12 grams at 460 calories, and it's a side.

Among fixed menu items, Subway's bowl carries the most fiber at 19 grams. Subway footnotes that section: the numbers assume the vegetables listed above, no dressing, no cheese. A build, not a scoop.

Cava's Falafel Crunch is the next fixed bowl at 18 grams, 860 calories, and 2,210 milligrams of sodium. Chick-fil-A's Chicken Tortilla Soup is also 18 grams, in 350 calories with 25 grams of protein and 1,115 milligrams of sodium. That's 190 fewer calories than the Subway bowl and 435 fewer milligrams of sodium. The soup page prints one 265 gram serving. It does not say cup.

## Which fast food item has the most fiber per calorie?

Chick-fil-A's Chicken Tortilla Soup, and the rest of this list isn't close. Eighteen grams in 350 calories is **5.1 grams of fiber per 100 kcal**.

Next is Subway's Veggie Patty Protein Bowl at 3.5, then the Chipotle veggie bowl at 3.4. Panera's Green Goddess Chicken Wrap is 3.0. The old density leader, Panera's black bean soup at 12.9 grams per 100 calories, is not in the September 2, 2026 guide.

Large guacamole looks better on this column than it does as lunch: 2.6 grams per 100 kcal, with 4 grams of protein. Starbucks' plain oatmeal is also 2.5 grams per 100 kcal, at 4 grams of fiber total. Density without grams is just a small bowl wearing a good ratio.

## How much fiber is in Wendy's baked potato and chili?

Ten grams for the pair: the U.S. menu feed lists the Plain Baked Potato at 7 grams and the Small Chili at 3 more.

Together the potato and Small Chili provide the table's 10 grams of fiber, 550 calories, 26 grams of protein, and 1,090 milligrams of sodium. Wendy's ships a Saltine Packet as a default component and publishes no nutrition line for it, so the page can't say whether the packet sits inside the 280 or on top.

The same feed lists Chili Cheese Potato at 8 grams, 490 calories, 21 grams of protein, and 830 milligrams of sodium. That's one published item, not a sum. The taco salad on that feed is also 8 grams, at 610 calories.

## How much fiber is in Panera's Mediterranean Veggie?

Panera's current U.S. guide, effective September 2, 2026, lists the whole Mediterranean Veggie on Tomato Basil at **8 grams of fiber**, not 10, plus 520 calories and 1,260 milligrams of sodium. Its Steel Cut Oatmeal lists 7 grams for 330 calories. Same fiber neighborhood, wildly different meal.

The Grab N Go wraps are the high line in that file: Green Goddess Chicken Wrap and Chicken Caesar Wrap at 14 grams, Frontega Chicken Wrap at 12.

## How to order fiber when this list goes stale

Menus move. These rules don't.

1. **Beans do the work.** Chipotle lists 7 grams of fiber per 4 ounces of black beans, 8 for pinto. Together they're 15 grams before anything else.
2. **Read the sodium.** A lot of the higher-fiber meals clear 1,000 milligrams against a [2,300-milligram Daily Value](https://www.fda.gov/food/nutrition-facts-label/daily-value-nutrition-and-supplement-facts-labels). The plain Starbucks oatmeal, Panera's steel-cut oatmeal, Dunkin's avocado toast, and Popeyes' regular red beans and rice do not.
3. **Match the build.** A custom bowl is only the number in the table if the scoops match the ounces on the chart.

A drive-thru gram count isn't the grocery label. [What 2.8 g and 5.6 g mean on real food](/good-source-of-fiber-label-meaning/) is the cutoff for beans, oats, bread, and an apple you buy yourself.
