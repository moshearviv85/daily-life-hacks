You're already in the lane. The nutrition poster is six feet up, the car behind you has opinions, and "chicken plus something with beans" is as specific as the plan got.

Fast food meals can clear 20 grams of protein and 10 grams of fiber in the same bag. Fourteen orders did, in the chain files checked on November 23, 2026. The chart above is those fourteen. The table is the same list with calories and sodium, because a protein number without the salt is how you talk yourself into a worse afternoon.

## Which fast-food meals actually give you at least 20 grams of protein and 10 grams of fiber?

Fourteen orders in public/data/high-fiber-fast-food-2026.csv clear 20 grams of protein and 10 grams of fiber. El Pollo Loco's Grande Avocado Chicken Bowl is 49 grams of protein and 11 grams of fiber. The Cool Wrap is 43 and 14. Chipotle's veggie bowl is 23 and 25, and Subway's Veggie Patty Protein Bowl is 22 and 19.

That's every qualifying row, not a highlight reel. The nachos, the Caesar wrap, the falafel bowl, and the sofritas bowl stay because they clear the line too. public/data/fastfood-protein-per-dollar-2026.csv prices other menu items, and those prices don't belong here.

## What the builds actually contain

Don't order "the high-fiber bowl" and hope the line cook shares your definition.

Chipotle's veggie burrito bowl here is brown rice, both beans, fajita vegetables, 4 ounces of guacamole, and romaine. No salsa. Skip a bean and it's a different meal. The sofritas bowl drops the pinto beans and the romaine: 23 grams of protein, 19 grams of fiber, 740 calories. Not the same bowl.

Cava's two bowls are curated items in the September 5, 2025 guide. Panera's three wraps are Grab N Go items in the guide effective September 2, 2026. Green Goddess and Chicken Caesar both sit at 32 grams of protein and 14 grams of fiber. Caesar is the saltier one, 1,510 milligrams of sodium versus 1,340.

El Pollo Loco's Grande Avocado Chicken Bowl leads protein at 49 grams, with 11 grams of fiber. The shredded chicken nachos also clear it, at 34 and 13, and they bring 1,090 calories. Nachos passed the cutoff. They didn't become a salad.

## Calories and sodium still sit on the receipt

The [FDA's Daily Value](https://www.fda.gov/food/nutrition-facts-label/daily-value-nutrition-and-supplement-facts-labels) is 28 grams of fiber and 50 grams of protein on a 2,000-calorie label, and 2,300 milligrams of sodium. Those are labeling references, not a target somebody set for your Tuesday.

Chick-fil-A's Chicken Tortilla Soup is the quiet one on calories: 25 grams of protein, 18 grams of fiber, 350 calories, 1,115 milligrams of sodium. The Grande Avocado Chicken Bowl is the loud one on salt: 49 grams of protein, 11 grams of fiber, 790 calories, 2,270 milligrams of sodium. Cava's Falafel Crunch is 24 and 18, at 860 calories and 2,210 milligrams of sodium.

Ten grams of fiber is about 36 percent of that 28-gram label value. The FDA treats 20 percent of a Daily Value or more as a general guide for a high amount of a nutrient. That describes the fiber column. It doesn't grade the sandwich, the nachos, or the rest of your day.

The longer fiber list, including orders that miss 20 grams of protein, is the [high-fiber fast food guide](/high-fiber-fast-food-options-guide/). This page is only the overlap.

## The drive-thru is still the expensive way to buy both

I priced a different set of menu items for protein per dollar, and the winner was a cup of chicken, not a bowl with beans. That ranking is [fast food protein per dollar](/fast-food-protein-per-dollar-ranked/). It doesn't tell you which of these fourteen is cheapest, because the price file and the fiber file aren't the same orders. Pasting one onto the other would be a cute chart and a fake one.

At the grocery store, dry beans still do this job for less money. Split peas lead the [beans that deliver protein and fiber per dollar](/beans-double-win-fiber-protein/). Cook the bag when you can.

Grocery price rules are on the [methodology page](/methodology/). These grams are the chain's nutrition file, read on November 23, 2026.

If you're at the speaker, match the build. A custom bowl hits the table only when the scoops match. Then read the sodium before you call it the responsible order. The chart shows which ones clear both lines. The receipt can still talk you out of a couple.
