The jar is full and dinner is not a fermentation project. That's the whole conflict. Sourdough discard recipes with no added yeast are the ones that use the jar for flavor and then let baking soda, baking powder, or a hot pan do the rest.

I'd rather put a number on the discard and a number on the clock than tell you to "use up your starter." Cold discard from an established starter is fine. It doesn't need to double first. If the starter is brand new, or the jar is moldy, pink, orange, or rotten, this list isn't the next step.

## What leavens the dough when you skip the yeast packet

Unfed discard is sour and tired. King Arthur's point, and it's the useful one, is that discard brings flavor and doesn't do much leavening on its own. You still have three ways to get a bake out of it.

**Baking soda plus the acid in the jar.** Pancakes and the big waffle batch foam as soon as the soda lands. Cook them. This isn't batter to park while you hunt for a spatula.

**Baking powder, which brings its own acid.** Pizza, tortillas, biscuits, brownies, and the quick cinnamon rolls. Lift without a rise you have to schedule, and a denser crumb than a dough that sat all afternoon. That's the trade.

**Heat and a thin shape.** Flatbread, crackers, and tortillas don't need to puff like bread. A lid can blister the surface with steam. It doesn't turn discard into a ripe starter.

None of this makes the jar yeast-free. The culture still has wild yeast in it. "No yeast" on this page means no commercial yeast and no rise you're waiting on. King Arthur's [sourdough naan](https://www.kingarthurbaking.com/recipes/sourdough-naan-recipe) is the recipe people mean when they say naan, and it adds 2 1/4 teaspoons of instant yeast to 114 grams of discard. Total time on that card is 2 hours 10 minutes. It's a good bread. It's not this list.

## Where the cup on the card is lying

The pancake batter foams the second the baking soda hits it. Cook it on medium-low. A dark outside and a wet middle means the pan was too hot, not that the jar needs more soda.

The waffle card is that same soda reaction at **1,000 grams**, scaled in **250 gram** steps. A quarter batch is just over this site's cup. Don't pour the full kilogram into the pancake skillet.

A few cups on other cards don't match ours. King Arthur's biscuits call **90 grams** a third of a cup. Ours is about 76 grams. Weigh the 90, then the 120 grams of flour, 60 grams of cold butter, 30 grams of milk, and **12 grams of baking powder**. That's their tablespoon. Bake at 450F, about 15 minutes.

Lisa Bass's cinnamon rolls call **250 grams** one cup. Ours is 227. She uses 2 teaspoons baking powder and 1 teaspoon baking soda, then 375F for 25 to 35 minutes. Her card also says 15 minutes of prep and a 45 minute total. Believe the oven range.

The [pizza crust](/easy-sourdough-discard-pizza-dough-no-yeast/) is the 227 gram baking-powder dough you can finish in about 40 minutes. Tortillas are the other 227 to 241 gram cup, about an hour, 60 to 80 seconds a side. Banana bread is **113 grams** at 325F, and the 1 hour 40 minute total includes 65 to 75 minutes in the oven. Brownies are **113 grams** and 22 to 26 minutes at 350F. The 2 hour 44 minute total is cooling and a fridge set. She wants that discard under 2 weeks old.

## Flat doughs, including the naan-style lid

Half a cup, **113 grams**, is one skillet flatbread in 10 to 15 minutes. Salt, a spoon of scallion, a spoon of flour, spread thin. For a naan-style round, same batter, lid on for the first minute so steam blisters the top. If it sticks, take the lid off and finish it plain. A lid isn't yeast.

The [rolled crackers](/sourdough-discard-crackers/) are **227 grams** of discard, **120 grams** of all-purpose flour, and **57 grams** of butter. Chill 30 minutes, roll to about 1/16 inch, 350F for 20 to 25 minutes. King Arthur prints 113 grams of golden wheat flour for that dough. 120 grams is their weight for a cup of all-purpose. The no-roll sheet skips the flour and takes about 65 minutes plus cooling at 325F. Pick the clock you have.

## Use the grams on the card, then cook it through

A bubbly cup is full of air. The [discard conversion guide](/how-to-measure-sourdough-discard-grams/) is why these rows are in grams. Stir, tare the bowl, pour. A 100% hydration jar is half flour and half water by weight. Change the jar's thickness and every batter in the table moves.

Discard still contains raw flour. Cook the center through before you decide you like it. And if you won't bake this week, [freeze measured portions and thaw them overnight](/how-long-does-sourdough-discard-last/). A week in the fridge is the planning window. Three months is the freezer one. The jar doesn't get safer because a recipe was short.
