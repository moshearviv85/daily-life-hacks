The shelf is full and the clock isn't. You don't have a soak going. You have bread, a jar of peanut butter, and a can you've been stepping around since Tuesday. These 20-minute high-fiber meals from pantry staples are eight plates I weighed, then priced. They run from 9.8 g to 14.8 g of fiber and from $0.89 to $1.51.

Fiber and the price per 100 g come from fiber-per-dollar-2026.csv. Protein grams per 100 g come from protein-per-dollar-2026.csv. shelf-stable-pantry-per-dollar-2026.csv has to match that protein-per-dollar rate, or the food never joins the plate. I add the parts, then round fiber and protein half up to a tenth and cost half up to the cent. The filenames stay in the sentence. This page doesn't link the files.

Nothing here needs an overnight soak. Canned beans, bread, peanut butter, and nuts are already edible. Oats, whole wheat spaghetti, and quinoa still want a pot. That pot is the 20 minutes.

## What $0.89 and $0.90 actually buy

Chickpea peanut toast is the Tuesday plate. Mash the chickpeas, spread the peanut butter, eat it over the sink if the table is a lost cause. Kidney beans and peanuts match that $0.89 receipt with a handful of peanuts instead of the spoon. Same bread weight. Different crunch.

Peanut toast with peanuts is the fiber floor, 9.8 g, and it's still $0.90. You're paying for more bread. If 9.8 g feels light next to the other plates, it's the light one. The receipt didn't get cheaper by shrinking the fiber. The $0.89 plates already took that spot.

Peanut spaghetti looks like a dare and eats like noodles. Toss the peanut butter into the hot pasta with a splash of the cooking water, then the almonds. The oat bowl ties that $0.90 and takes the fiber crown at 14.8 g. Breakfast if you start the pot. Dinner if the can opener went missing. It isn't delicate.

## Where the receipt climbs

Chickpea sunflower toast leaves the 90-cent cluster at $1.04. Sunflower kernels aren't the expensive part. The extra bread is.

Black beans and almonds hit 14.8 g again, at $1.13, and the bean scoop is only 90 g. I kept it there on purpose. A heavier scoop of this can, next to bread and almonds, walks out of the band the other plates sit in. The whole can is a different plate.

Quinoa, almonds, and toast is the $1.51 end. The fiber, 14.5 g, isn't higher than the $0.90 oat bowl. You're paying for the grain, not for more fiber.
