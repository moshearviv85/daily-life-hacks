## Body

Hey,

We spent last month pricing out something nobody actually publishes: what a day of 30 grams of fiber costs, depending on where you get it. One consistent set of Walmart prices, USDA nutrition numbers, five different days.

**The cheapest day came to $0.62.** Oats for breakfast, split pea soup, pinto beans and rice for dinner, popcorn for a snack. That's 31.9 grams of fiber for sixty-two cents.

**The most expensive day came to $14.42.** Starbucks oatmeal, a Wendy's baked potato, a large chili, a Taco Bell bean burrito. 31.0 grams of fiber.

Same fiber. Twenty-three times the price.

The chili is honestly good and I'm not going to pretend otherwise. But it's worth knowing that the $4.39 it costs buys you 9 grams of fiber, and that same $4.39 spent on dry split peas buys you about 310 grams.

Before you assume the answer is "eat beans forever like a monk," the realistic day in the middle landed at $1.99. Oats, a banana, peanut butter toast, an apple, black beans and broccoli. 32.1 grams. That's the one most people could actually live on.

**The rest of the numbers:**

- [What a day of 30 grams of fiber actually costs](https://www.daily-life-hacks.com/what-30-grams-of-fiber-costs-per-day/) — all five days, meal by meal, with the receipts
- [Fiber per dollar, 53 foods ranked](https://www.daily-life-hacks.com/fiber-per-dollar-cheapest-high-fiber-foods/) — whole wheat flour wins at 77.8 grams per dollar, split peas are right behind at 71.0
- [The fiber-per-dollar calculator](https://www.daily-life-hacks.com/tools/fiber-per-dollar-calculator/) — put in your own prices, it does the math against our data
- [And the same treatment for protein](https://www.daily-life-hacks.com/what-50-grams-of-protein-costs-per-day/) — $0.82 to $9.97 for 50 grams, which is a smaller gap than fiber but still absurd

Every number above comes from a public CSV. If you think one of them is wrong, hit reply and tell me which one. We've already corrected six values in the fiber data after somebody checked our work, and the correction note is still on the page.

David
